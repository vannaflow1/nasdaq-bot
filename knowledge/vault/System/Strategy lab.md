# Strategy lab
Complete intraday NQ strategies (entry, filter, stop, target, time exit) as JSON specs, backtested on 1-minute NQ 2014-2023 with pessimistic fills and costs; must beat random entries, survive a stress test and pass a locked 2024-2026 exam (max 10 exams/week). Passing strategies are forward-tested every evening. Volta designs strategies itself (every second curiosity turn). 20 Oct 2026: mechanical fades of yesterday's high/low, Asia/London, VWAP bands and the midnight open all lose (stops run ~70%), with or without confluence.

# Does innovativeness reduce startup survival rates?
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (prop mission)  ·  [source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0883902614000913/pdf)

## What Volta learned
Innovativeness measured before outcomes can predict lower survival odds for startups, especially when founders are more risk‑seeking. Early innovation may increase downside risk before any market advantage materialises.

## Why it matters (or not)
Shows that high‑risk, innovative strategies can increase early drawdowns, which matters for prop‑account drawdown limits.

## Concepts
ex‑ante risk, drawdown exposure, risk appetite, early‑stage volatility, survival probability

# Economic Incentives and Social Preferences: Substitutes or Complements?
★★★★☆  ·  OpenAlex (prop mission)  ·  [source](http://repec.deps.unisi.it/quaderni/617.pdf)

## What Volta learned
Explicit monetary incentives can weaken or strengthen intrinsic motivations depending on framing, perceived autonomy, and informational signals. When incentives are poorly designed they may crowd out the trader's intrinsic drive for disciplined, optimal performance, reducing overall payoff. Well‑crafted incentives that respect autonomy and signal supportive oversight can complement intrinsic motivation, enhancing performance.

## Why it matters (or not)
Prop‑trading evaluations are essentially incentive contracts; their design can affect the trader's internal discipline and risk‑taking behavior.

crowding out, crowding in, autonomy framing, intrinsic motivation, incentive design

## What Volta learned
The paper builds a simulation framework that generates implied volatility for American options along stock price paths generated by a jump hidden Markov model, using fitted parametric or neural IV surfaces and a Heston‑style variance update.

## Why it matters (or not)
Provides insight into IV modeling and simulation but shows limited practical forecasting benefit.

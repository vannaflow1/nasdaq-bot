# Stock Market Uncertainty and the Stock-Bond Return Relation
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.17615/6dzv-pw48)

## What Volta learned
This older study links daily stock and Treasury bond moves to stock-market uncertainty, measured by index option implied volatility and unusually high trading volume. When implied volatility is high, stocks and bonds tend to move less together in the following period, and on days when implied volatility jumps, bonds tend to do well relative to stocks (a flight to safety). In plain terms: bonds diversify a stock portfolio best exactly when fear is high. The abstract gives no specific numbers.

## Why it matters (or not)
A cross-asset portfolio finding about stock-bond correlation, not a tool for next-day NQ range or QQQ direction, and we have no bond data to check it.

# The Variance Risk Premium in NIFTY Index Options: Smaller than It Looks, Priced in the Wings, and Paid for Tail Risk
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23880589)

## What Volta learned
The study finds that the variance risk premium (VRP) in NIFTY index options is much smaller than raw implied‑vs‑realized volatility gaps suggest, because a large share of realized variance comes from overnight moves and the premium is concentrated in OTM options, reflecting compensation for tail risk.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper focuses on Indian index options and methodology rather than US futures, offering limited direct insight for a Nasdaq/S&P trader.

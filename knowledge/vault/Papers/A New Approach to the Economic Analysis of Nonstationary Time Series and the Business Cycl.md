# A New Approach to the Economic Analysis of Nonstationary Time Series and the Business Cycle
★☆☆☆☆  ·  Must-read (curated)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.2307/1912559)

## What Volta learned
The paper presents a new approach to analyzing nonstationary time series, focusing on discrete shifts in growth rates. It applies this technique to the US business cycle, suggesting that recessions are characterized by a shift from positive to negative growth rates. The model estimates a typical recession results in a 3 percent permanent drop in GNP.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper is more focused on macroeconomic analysis and does not directly relate to trading strategies or models.

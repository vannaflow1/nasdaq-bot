# A joint quantile and expected shortfall regression framework
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/electronic-journal-of-statistics/volume-13/issue-1/A-joint-quantile-and-expected-shortfall-regression-framework/10.1214/19-EJS1560.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The paper introduces a new regression framework that jointly models the quantile and Expected Shortfall (ES) of a response variable given a set of covariates. This framework allows for M- and Z-estimation of the joint regression parameters, with a focus on M-estimation due to numerical stability issues with Z-estimation.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper is related to risk modeling and could be useful for traders or quants interested in backtesting and forecasting ES, but it does not directly provide trading strategies or models.

quantile regression, expected shortfall, m-estimation

# COVID-19 pandemic and the crude oil market risk: hedging options with non-energy financial innovations
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://jfin-swufe.springeropen.com/counter/pdf/10.1186/s40854-021-00253-1)

## What Volta learned
The paper finds that non-energy ETFs can serve as effective hedges for crude oil risk, especially during the COVID-19 pandemic, using a multivariate volatility model.

## Why it matters (or not)
Focuses on oil and ETFs, not on Nasdaq/S&P futures or options, so limited usefulness for retail equity traders.

## Concepts
hedging effectiveness, multivariate volatility, structural breaks, non-energy etfs, crude oil risk

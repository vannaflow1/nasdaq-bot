# OPTIMAL INVESTMENT STRATEGIES FOR CONTROLLING DRAWDOWNS
★★★☆☆  ·  Must-read (curated)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9965.1993.tb00044.x)

## What Volta learned
The paper derives an optimal risky‑asset allocation when an investor must keep wealth above a stochastic floor equal to a fixed fraction α of the historical maximum wealth. The optimal exposure is proportional to the surplus (W‑αM). When wealth is at its all‑time high (W=M) the floor grows faster than wealth, so exposure to risk falls; when wealth is near the floor, exposure rises.

## Why it matters (or not)
Provides a theoretically grounded method for scaling risky‑asset exposure based on drawdown, useful for position sizing and risk management.

## Concepts
drawdown constraint, surplus‑based allocation, crra utility, stochastic floor, risk exposure scaling

## Idea
Scale daily exposure to Nasdaq futures (or QQQ) in proportion to the surplus (current equity curve minus α times its running maximum). Set α≈0.8 and exposure = k·(W‑αM)/W, with k calibrated to target a given volatility.

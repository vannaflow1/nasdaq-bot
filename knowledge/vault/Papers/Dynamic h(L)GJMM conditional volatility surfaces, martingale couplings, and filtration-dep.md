# Dynamic h(L)GJMM: conditional volatility surfaces, martingale couplings, and filtration-dependent option pricing
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23885551)

## What Volta learned
The paper presents a joint mixture model that fits a vanilla volatility surface with a linear program, producing a martingale price process whose augmented state (price and latent mixture cell) is Markov and yields conditional implied volatility surfaces in closed form. The authors investigate the shape of these conditional smiles, noting that the calibrated model can produce downward‑opening (concave) smiles, but that using wider jump windows, KL projection or entropic calibration reduces this effect.

## Why it matters (or not)
Only the abstract is available; the paper is theoretical and lacks concrete empirical results for Nasdaq/S&P futures or options.

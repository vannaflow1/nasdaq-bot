# The Economic Limits of Bitcoin and the Blockchain
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (prop mission)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.3386/w24717)

## What Volta learned
The paper shows that security in proof‑of‑work systems relies on a balance between ongoing reward flows and one‑time attack benefits, with attack costs rising sharply with required hashpower. This creates a natural asymmetry: small, frequent losses are cheap, while large, rare gains (or attacks) are prohibitively expensive.

## Why it matters (or not)
The economics of attack costs map loosely onto prop‑trading risk limits, but the context is blockchain security, not futures markets.

## Concepts
flow vs stock incentives, majority attack as gambler's ruin, convex payoff structure, threshold‑based risk, resource‑limited attacks

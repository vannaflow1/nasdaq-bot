# How Do Exchanges Select Stocks for Option Listing?
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2004.00638.x)

## What Volta learned
Exchanges tend to list options on stocks with high trading volume, volatility, and market capitalization. The importance of these factors has shifted over time, with volatility becoming more prominent after 1980. There is no evidence that volatility declines with option introduction.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper discusses factors influencing option listing, which is somewhat relevant but does not provide direct insights for trading or quant strategies.

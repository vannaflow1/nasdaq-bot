## What Volta learned
The global financial stress index (GFSI) has strong predictive power for long-term equity market volatility, outperforming other indicators like VIX and economic policy uncertainty. GFSI is particularly effective during times of high global financial stress, such as the COVID-19 pandemic. This index can help policymakers and investors make better risk-aversion strategies.

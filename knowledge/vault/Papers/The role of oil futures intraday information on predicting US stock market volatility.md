# The role of oil futures intraday information on predicting US stock market volatility
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2096232020300494/pdf)

## What Volta learned
Oil futures intraday information can help predict US stock market volatility. Using oil futures realized volatility in a HAR (Heterogeneous Autoregressive) framework improves predictive ability. Multivariate HAR models outperform univariate models in forecasting volatility.

## Why it matters (or not)
The abstract relates to predicting stock market volatility using oil futures information, which might be useful for traders but does not directly provide a trading strategy or model.

## Concepts
har framework, oil futures, [[stock market volatility]], [[realized volatility]]

## Idea
Incorporate oil futures realized volatility into a multivariate HAR model to forecast US stock market volatility, particularly for indices like the S&P 500 or Nasdaq.

## What Volta learned
The paper shows an LSTM model using multiple market inputs can predict a crash in Indonesia's composite index one day ahead with about 78% accuracy.

## Why it matters (or not)
Focuses on an emerging market index and deep‑learning crash prediction; not directly applicable to US futures/options markets.

# Risk Premia in the Bitcoin Market
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.15195)

## What Volta learned
Bitcoin is far more volatile than the S&P 500 and its options carry a larger variance risk premium (sellers of volatility are paid more). Most of the S&P 500's equity premium comes from mildly negative return days, while for Bitcoin those moderate down moves explain only about a third of its premium. Bitcoin's premia shift between two regimes: in calm markets investors pay more for variance and upside, and in volatile markets the premium is more balanced and smaller.

## Why it matters (or not)
The comparison with the S&P 500 premium is mildly informative, but the paper is about Bitcoin options and gives no rule for NQ/QQQ trading.

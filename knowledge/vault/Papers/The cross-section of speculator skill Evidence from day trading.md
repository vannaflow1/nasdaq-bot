# The cross-section of speculator skill: Evidence from day trading
★★☆☆☆  ·  Must-read (curated)  ·  [source](https://escholarship.org/content/qt7k75v0qx/qt7k75v0qx.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The paper studies a large sample of day‑trading accounts and finds a wide cross‑section of ability: a small fraction consistently earn positive risk‑adjusted returns while the majority underperform after costs. Skill appears persistent over months and is linked to higher turnover and larger position sizes.

## Why it matters (or not)
Provides insight into how many day traders actually have skill, but offers no actionable models or signals for trading.

## Concepts
speculator skill distribution, performance persistence, cost-adjusted returns, turnover, risk‑adjusted performance

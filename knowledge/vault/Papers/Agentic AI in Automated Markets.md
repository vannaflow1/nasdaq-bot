# Agentic AI in Automated Markets
★☆☆☆☆  ·  Frontier (arXiv, last 45 days)  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22522v2)

## What Volta learned
The paper builds reinforcement‑learning agents that mimic three trader types—rational arbitrageur, manipulator, and herd‑following retail—within a simulated crypto market and shows they generate different stress patterns and Sharpe‑ratio dynamics.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study focuses on crypto hourly data and abstract agent behavior, offering little direct insight for trading Nasdaq or S&P futures/options.

## Concepts
agentic reinforcement learning, behavioral archetypes, market stress episodes, shap interpretability, automated market makers

# Are There Structural Breaks in Realized Volatility?
★★★★☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://www.economics.utoronto.ca/public/workingPapers/tecipa-304.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The paper investigates structural breaks in realized volatility (RV) of the S&P 500 index using a Bayesian approach. It finds strong evidence of a structural break in early 1997, mainly due to a reduction in the variance of log-volatility.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper is relevant for traders and quants as it studies the volatility of the S&P 500 index, which is a widely traded and benchmarked index.

## Concepts
[[realized volatility]], [[structural break]], bayesian approach, [[HAR model]]

## Idea
Test if incorporating a structural break in a HAR model improves out-of-sample density forecasts of log-volatility for the S&P 500 index.

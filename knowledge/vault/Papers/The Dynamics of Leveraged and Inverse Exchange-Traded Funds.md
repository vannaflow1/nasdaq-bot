# The Dynamics of Leveraged and Inverse Exchange-Traded Funds
★★★☆☆  ·  Must-read (curated)  ·  [source](http://onlinetradersforum.com/attachments/leveraged-etfs-pdf.4272/)

## What Volta learned
Leveraged and inverse ETFs have unique characteristics due to their embedded leverage, which can lead to increased market volatility and liquidity impact, particularly towards the close. Their structure can result in value destruction for buy-and-hold investors due to path-dependent options and high transaction costs.

## Why it matters (or not)
The abstract provides insights into the dynamics of leveraged and inverse ETFs, which can be useful for traders and investors, but its direct application to trading strategies or risk management is limited.

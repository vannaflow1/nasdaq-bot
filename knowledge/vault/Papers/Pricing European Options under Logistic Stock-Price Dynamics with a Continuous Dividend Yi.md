# Pricing European Options under Logistic Stock-Price Dynamics with a Continuous Dividend Yield
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://www.irejournals.com/formatedpaper/1724119.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The paper develops a no‑arbitrage option pricing model where the underlying follows a stochastic logistic process with a continuous dividend yield, using a state‑dependent market price of risk and solving the resulting PDE via Crank‑Nicolson and a closed‑form transformation.

## Why it matters (or not)
Only theoretical option pricing with logistic dynamics; no empirical results or direct trading implications for Nasdaq/S&P futures.

## Concepts
logistic stock dynamics, incomplete market pricing, state‑dependent market price of risk, feynman‑kac pde, dividend yield effect

# All in the family Nesting symmetric and asymmetric GARCH models
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0304405X9400821H/pdf)

## What Volta learned
The paper proposes a unified GARCH framework that nests many symmetric and asymmetric GARCH specifications and allows for formal nested tests of different asymmetry forms. It finds daily US stock returns favor a model where conditional volatility depends on a shifted absolute shock raised to the 1.5 power plus past volatility.

## Why it matters (or not)
Only a theoretical GARCH extension is described; no concrete results for Nasdaq/S&P futures or options are provided.

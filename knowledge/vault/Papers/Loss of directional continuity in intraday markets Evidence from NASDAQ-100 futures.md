# Loss of directional continuity in intraday markets: Evidence from NASDAQ-100 futures
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://businessperspectives.org/images/pdf/applications/publishing/templates/article/assets/25687/IMFI_2026_04_Perdomo.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The study defines a ‘loss of directional continuity’ (LDC) candlestick pattern in Nasdaq‑100 futures that occurs when a breakout is followed by a quick re‑entry into the prior range, a small body, and a long wick in the breakout direction, and finds that such events lead to 12‑24% lower short‑term price continuation than ordinary breakouts.

## Why it matters (or not)
Provides insight into a specific high‑frequency pattern but cannot be directly backtested with the available daily data.

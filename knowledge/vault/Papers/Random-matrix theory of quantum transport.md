# Random-matrix theory of quantum transport
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (prop mission)  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/9612179)

## What Volta learned
The paper reviews how random-matrix ensembles (circular, Poisson, DMPK) describe the full statistical distribution of transmission eigenvalues in mesoscopic conductors, linking universal conductance fluctuations, shot noise, and localization to eigenvalue correlations.

## Why it matters (or not)
The theory is about quantum transport statistics, not directly applicable to NQ futures prop‑trading or position‑sizing under drawdown limits.

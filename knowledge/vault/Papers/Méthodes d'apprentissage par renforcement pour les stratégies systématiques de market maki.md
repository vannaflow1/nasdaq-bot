## What Volta learned
The paper explores using reinforcement learning for market making strategies, focusing on improving decision-making for market makers who provide liquidity by quoting buy and sell prices. It proposes modifications to LSTM structures and neural networks to estimate execution probabilities, enhancing the efficiency of reinforcement learning.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper is related to trading and market making but does not directly provide testable trading ideas or concrete numbers for retail traders.

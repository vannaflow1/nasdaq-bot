## What Volta learned
The paper proposes a three‑gate 'admission clock' to decide when a new crypto‑asset instrument can be issued, emphasizing that the market‑regime gate should use an exogenous signal—specifically, the count of small equity‑issuance deals—as a leading indicator of capital appetite, rather than internal crypto‑price‑based factors.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study focuses on crypto issuance timing and operator heuristics, with no direct link to Nasdaq/S&P futures, options, or tradable quantitative models.

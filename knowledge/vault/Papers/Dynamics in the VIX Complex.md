# Dynamics in the VIX Complex
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.3723728)

## What Volta learned
This paper models the 'VIX complex': the VIX itself, the curve of VIX futures (summarised by its level, slope and curvature), and the exchange-traded products that hold those futures. It finds evidence that buying and selling in VIX ETPs moves the VIX futures curve, but no evidence that it moves the VIX index itself. In plain terms, VIX ETP flows can distort the futures you trade, while the spot fear gauge is still driven by S&P 500 option prices.

## Why it matters (or not)
It is about S&P 500 volatility products, not NQ/QQQ options, and its main point (ETP flows move VIX futures but not the VIX) has no direct day-trading use for Nasdaq; I worked from the abstract only because no full text was available.

# Put-Call Parity Violations and Market Frictions in China's SSE 50 ETF Options Market: Evidence from the AI Boom of 2023
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://journals.zeuspress.org/index.php/conference/article/download/1507/1449)

## What Volta learned
In China's SSE 50 ETF options market during 2023, calls were consistently overpriced relative to puts, driven by retail demand for AI‑related bullish bets and constrained arbitrage due to high margin requirements and short‑selling limits.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study focuses on Chinese ETF options and sentiment‑driven mispricing, offering little direct insight for trading US/Nasdaq futures or options.

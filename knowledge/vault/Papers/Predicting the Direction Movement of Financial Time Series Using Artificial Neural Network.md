# Predicting the Direction Movement of Financial Time Series Using Artificial Neural Network and Support Vector Machine
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://downloads.hindawi.com/journals/complexity/2021/2906463.pdf)

## What Volta learned
This study compares the performance of Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) and Support Vector Machines (SVM) in predicting the direction of daily stock price index movements for several international indexes.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study focuses on direction prediction of stock indexes using ANN and SVM, which is somewhat relevant but not directly applicable to Nasdaq/S&P index futures and options trading.

## Concepts
ann, svm, [[technical indicators]], [[financial time series]]

## Idea
Can an ANN model outperform an SVM model in predicting the direction of daily Nasdaq-100 index movements using technical indicators derived from trading data?

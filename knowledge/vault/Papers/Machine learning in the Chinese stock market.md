# Machine learning in the Chinese stock market
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21003743/pdf)

## What Volta learned
This study uses machine learning to predict stock returns in the Chinese market, finding liquidity to be a key predictor and retail investors' presence to impact short-term predictability, especially for small stocks.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study focuses on the Chinese stock market, which may have different dynamics than the US market, but the concepts of liquidity and retail investor impact could be relevant to traders in other markets.

# A Deep Learning-Driven Operator Splitting Scheme for Free Boundary Problems: An Application to American Option Pricing in Incomplete Markets
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.36921)

## What Volta learned
The paper builds a deep‑learning (physics‑informed neural net) method to price high‑dimensional American options when you have transaction costs, stochastic liquidity and two‑factor stochastic volatility, turning the problem into a 5‑D variational inequality and solving it by splitting each time step into a PDE solved by a PINN and a projection enforcing early‑exercise.

## Why it matters (or not)
The method is advanced and not directly applicable to daily retail trading or back‑testing with the available Volta columns.

# Control variates for variance-reduced ratio of means estimators
★☆☆☆☆  ·  Hunt: variance reduction control variates backtest evaluation  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13504v3)

## What Volta learned
The paper extends the classic control‑variates technique to estimate ratios of expectations (e.g., a numerator and denominator) by applying control variates to both parts and jointly optimizing their coefficients to minimise the estimator’s variance.

## Why it matters (or not)
The work is about Monte‑Carlo variance reduction for simulation studies, not directly applicable to trading or market data.

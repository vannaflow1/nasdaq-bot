# Probabilistic Limit Order Book Forecasting with Conditional Diffusion Models
★★☆☆☆  ·  arXiv  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25495v1)

## What Volta learned
The paper introduces LOBGrad, a conditional diffusion model for probabilistic limit order book (LOB) forecasting, combining an autoregressive RNN encoder with a diffusion-based generator. It establishes statistical guarantees and shows improved performance over baselines in experiments on BTC/USDT, GOOGL, and META. LOBGrad reproduces key stylized facts of LOB microstructure and yields calibrated predictive intervals.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper is related to market microstructure and forecasting, but its direct application to retail trading or common indices like Nasdaq/S&P is not clear.

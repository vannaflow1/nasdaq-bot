# A CNN-LSTM-Based Model to Forecast Stock Prices
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://downloads.hindawi.com/journals/complexity/2020/6622927.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The paper proposes a CNN-LSTM model for stock price forecasting, which combines the strengths of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for feature extraction and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks for time series prediction. The model is compared to other machine learning models, including MLP, CNN, RNN, LSTM, and CNN-RNN, using daily stock price data from 1991 to 2020. The CNN-LSTM model shows the highest prediction accuracy.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper is related to machine learning and stock price forecasting, but it does not specifically focus on volatility, options, or risk management, which are key areas of interest for a trader or quant.

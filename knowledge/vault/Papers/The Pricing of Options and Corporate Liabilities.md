# The Pricing of Options and Corporate Liabilities
★★★☆☆  ·  Must-read (curated)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.1086/260062)

## What Volta learned
The paper discusses a theoretical valuation formula for options and its applicability to corporate liabilities. It suggests that if options are correctly priced, it shouldn't be possible to make risk-free profits by combining long and short positions in options and stocks.

## Why it matters (or not)
The abstract does not provide specific testable trading ideas or direct application to Nasdaq/S&P index futures and options, but it introduces a foundational concept in finance.

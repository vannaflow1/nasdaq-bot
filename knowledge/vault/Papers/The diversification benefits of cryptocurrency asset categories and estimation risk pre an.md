# The diversification benefits of cryptocurrency asset categories and estimation risk: pre and post Covid-19
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.1080/1351847x.2022.2033806)

## What Volta learned
Cryptocurrencies can provide significant diversification benefits in a portfolio, especially for investors with lower risk aversion. These benefits persist even in uncertain economic environments like the post-Covid-19 period.

## Why it matters (or not)
The abstract is focused on portfolio diversification using cryptocurrencies, which may not be directly applicable to Nasdaq/S&P index futures and options trading.

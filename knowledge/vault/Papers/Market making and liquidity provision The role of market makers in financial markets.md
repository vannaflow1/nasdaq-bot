## What Volta learned
Market makers play a crucial role in financial markets by providing continuous liquidity through bid and ask quotes. They help with price discovery, reduce volatility, and improve transaction cost efficiency. Their risk management strategies include hedging, mitigating adverse selection, and stress testing.

## Why it matters (or not)

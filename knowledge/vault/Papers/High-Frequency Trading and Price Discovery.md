# High-Frequency Trading and Price Discovery
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://www.econstor.eu/bitstream/10419/154035/1/ecbwp1602.pdf)

## What Volta learned
High-frequency traders help prices find their correct level: their aggressive (liquidity-taking) orders trade in the direction of lasting price changes and against temporary mispricings, even on the most volatile days. Their passive quotes, on the other hand, tend to get picked off by better-informed traders. Their trading direction predicts price moves only over seconds and is tied to public news, market moves and order book imbalance.

## Why it matters (or not)
Explains microstructure over seconds, far shorter than the bot's daily horizon, and needs order-level data we do not have.

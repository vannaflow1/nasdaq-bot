# Some Control Variates for exotic options
★☆☆☆☆  ·  Hunt: variance reduction control variates backtest evaluation  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.4675v1)

## What Volta learned
The paper shows how to pick optimal linear combinations of the log‑returns (X_i) as control variates to cut variance when Monte‑Carlo pricing Asian or lookback options, proving the variance reduction equals 1‑sum of squared correlations.

## Why it matters (or not)
It is a theoretical variance‑reduction technique for exotic‑option pricing, not directly usable for trading Nasdaq/S&P futures or options.

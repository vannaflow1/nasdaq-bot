# Frictions as a Constraint on Tax Planning
☆☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.262174)

## What Volta learned
This law-review article argues that narrow tax reforms aimed at specific tax-avoidance tricks work only when 'frictions' (fees, accounting or regulatory treatment, credit risk) make the workaround costly, and that sharp, all-or-nothing frictions deter end-runs best. It compares two US rules on derivatives, Section 1259 (constructive sales of appreciated stock) and Section 1260 (using derivatives to mimic hedge-fund ownership), which use almost the same wording yet drew different taxpayer responses because of differing frictions.

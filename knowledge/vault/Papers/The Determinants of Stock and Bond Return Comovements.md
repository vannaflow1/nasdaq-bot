# The Determinants of Stock and Bond Return Comovements
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.3386/w15260)

## What Volta learned
Using a model that ties stock-bond co-movement to interest rates, inflation, growth, cash flows, risk aversion, uncertainty and liquidity, the authors find that macro fundamentals explain little of how stocks and bonds move together; liquidity measures matter more. Macro factors help explain bond volatility, while the variance premium is key for explaining stock volatility. The model mostly fails to fit the stock-bond covariance itself. The abstract gives no specific numbers.

## Why it matters (or not)
Cross-asset, low-frequency macro research; the note that the variance premium drives stock volatility is consistent with what we track, but nothing here gives a daily NQ/QQQ signal.

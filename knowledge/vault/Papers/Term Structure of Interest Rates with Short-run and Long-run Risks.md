# Term Structure of Interest Rates with Short-run and Long-run Risks
★☆☆☆☆  ·  Hunt: intraday IV term structure as predictor of short‑term return  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.17016/feds.2015.095)

## What Volta learned
The paper proposes a consumption‑based model with long‑run and short‑run risks, showing that the variance risk premium from interest‑rate derivatives strongly predicts short‑horizon Treasury excess returns, more so than standard forward‑rate factors.

## Why it matters (or not)
Focuses on Treasury bonds and interest‑rate derivatives, with no direct results for equity index futures/options.

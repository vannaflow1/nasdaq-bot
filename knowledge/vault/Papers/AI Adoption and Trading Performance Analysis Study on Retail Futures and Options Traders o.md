# AI Adoption and Trading Performance Analysis: Study on Retail Futures and Options Traders of Karnataka
☆☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://ijcope.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/AI-Adoption-and-Trading-Performance-Analysis-Study-on-Retail-Futures-and-Options-Traders-of-Karnataka-1.pdf)

## What Volta learned
This survey asked 401 retail futures-and-options traders in six cities of Karnataka, India, on 1-5 agreement scales, about their use of AI trading tools, the quality of their decisions and their own trading performance. In a structural model, AI adoption was linked to better self-rated decision quality and performance (direct effect 0.49, with about one third of the total effect running through decision quality), and the link was stronger for traders with more financial literacy. Performance was self-reported, not measured from actual profit and loss, while the paper itself notes that regulator data show most Indian retail derivative traders lose money.

## Why it matters (or not)
Not relevant to NQ/QQQ decisions: it is an opinion survey with self-rated performance, which cannot show whether AI tools actually make traders money.

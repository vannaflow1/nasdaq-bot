# Volatility risk premia and exchange rate predictability
★★★☆☆  ·  Must-read (curated)  ·  [source](http://hdl.handle.net/10044/1/39815)

## What Volta learned
The paper explores the concept of volatility risk premia in predicting currency returns and creating a profitable trading strategy. It finds that selling high insurance-cost currencies and buying low insurance-cost currencies can generate desirable returns and diversification benefits.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper is related to finance and trading, but its focus on currency markets and volatility risk premia makes it less directly applicable to Nasdaq/S&P index futures and options trading.

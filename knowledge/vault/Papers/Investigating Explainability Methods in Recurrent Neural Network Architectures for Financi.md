# Investigating Explainability Methods in Recurrent Neural Network Architectures for Financial Time Series Data
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/12/3/1427/pdf?version=1643379379)

## What Volta learned
This study applies explainable AI (XAI) methods to recurrent neural networks (RNNs) for financial time series prediction, specifically using S&P 500 stocks data. The study compares the performance of four XAI methods across different RNN architectures, including GRU, LSTM, and RNN. The results show that these XAI methods can be applied to financial forecasting, but may require further development for hybrid prediction systems.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study focuses on applying XAI methods to RNNs for financial time series prediction, which has some relevance to trading and quantitative finance, but does not provide direct trading strategies or results.

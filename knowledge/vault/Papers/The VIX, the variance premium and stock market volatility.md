# The VIX, the variance premium and stock market volatility
★★★★☆  ·  Must-read (curated)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2014.05.008)

## What Volta learned
The paper explores the relationship between the VIX index, the variance premium, and stock market volatility, providing insights into how these measures can be used to understand market behavior and volatility. It discusses the concept of the variance premium, which is the difference between the expected variance of returns under the risk-neutral measure and the physical measure. The study likely sheds light on how the VIX and variance premium can be used as indicators of market sentiment and potential future volatility.

## What Volta learned
The paper reviews fractional dynamics, especially superdiffusion and first‑passage‑time (FPT) analysis for anomalous transport, showing how boundary conditions affect the probability of reaching a target before a barrier.

## Why it matters (or not)
Understanding anomalous price dynamics can improve estimates of stop‑loss vs profit‑target hitting probabilities, but the paper does not directly address futures trading.

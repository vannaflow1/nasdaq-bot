# Limited Arbitrage and Short Sales Restrictions: Evidence from the Options Markets
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.3386/w9423)

## What Volta learned
The paper investigates put-call parity violations in the presence of short sale restrictions. It finds that violations are asymmetric, related to the rebate rate spread, and persist even with transactions costs. The study also shows that violations predict future stock returns, with cumulative abnormal returns exceeding 65% over a 2.5-year period.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper provides useful insights into the relationship between short sale restrictions and options pricing, but its direct application to trading strategies is limited.

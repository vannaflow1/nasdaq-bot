# Empirical asset pricing via machine learning: evidence from the European stock market
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1057/s41260-021-00237-x.pdf)

## What Volta learned
Machine learning models, particularly neural networks and support vector machines, can improve the prediction of European stock returns and outperform linear benchmark models. These models benefit from interactions and nonlinear effects, but require careful training and tuning to avoid overfitting. The most important predictors are price trends and fundamental signals from valuation ratios.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper focuses on European stock markets and doesn't specifically mention Nasdaq/S&P index futures and options, but the concepts can still be applied to US markets.

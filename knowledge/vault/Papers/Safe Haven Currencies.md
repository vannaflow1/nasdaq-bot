# Safe Haven Currencies
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://academic.oup.com/rof/article-pdf/14/3/385/26300055/rfq007.pdf)

## What Volta learned
Ranaldo and Söderlind used high-frequency exchange-rate data from 1993 to 2008 and a factor model linking currencies to stocks, bonds, volatility and liquidity. They find that the Swiss franc and Japanese yen strengthen against the US dollar when US stocks fall, US bond prices rise and FX volatility increases. This safe-haven behaviour shows up from a few hours to several days, grows non-linearly with volatility and in crises, and was especially strong for the yen in 2008. Worked from the abstract only (no full text in the repo).

## Why it matters (or not)
A yen or franc rally can flag risk-off moods that hit the Nasdaq, but this is an FX paper and Volta has no currency data, so it cannot be tested or used directly.

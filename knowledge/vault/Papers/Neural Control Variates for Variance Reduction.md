# Neural Control Variates for Variance Reduction
★☆☆☆☆  ·  Hunt: variance reduction control variates backtest evaluation  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.00159v2)

## What Volta learned
The paper proposes using neural networks as flexible control variates to cut Monte‑Carlo estimation variance, and adds a constraint to stop the network from over‑fitting when sample sizes are tiny.

## Why it matters (or not)
The work is about generic Monte‑Carlo integration, not about market data, pricing, or futures/options, so it offers little direct insight for traders.

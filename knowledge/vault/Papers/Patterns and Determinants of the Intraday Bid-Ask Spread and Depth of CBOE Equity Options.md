# Patterns and Determinants of the Intraday Bid-Ask Spread and Depth of CBOE Equity Options
★★★☆☆  ·  Hunt: low dealer gamma intraday volatility impact  ·  [source](https://harvest.usask.ca/bitstream/10388/8317/1/CHEN-THESIS-2018.pdf)

## What Volta learned
Option bid‑ask spreads are highest at market open and drop sharply within the first 90 minutes, then stay relatively stable; spread widens again near market close. Factors widening spreads include higher underlying stock price, larger stock spread, longer time‑to‑expiry, lower depth, higher volatility, and VIX level.

## Why it matters (or not)
Provides useful timing insight for options traders but lacks direct daily metrics for back‑testing with our dataset.

## Concepts
l‑shaped intraday spread, option depth, underlying stock price effect, vix impact, time‑of‑day liquidity

## Idea
Avoid initiating new equity option positions during the first 30‑minute interval of the trading day; instead, wait until after 10:00 EST to benefit from narrower spreads and higher depth.

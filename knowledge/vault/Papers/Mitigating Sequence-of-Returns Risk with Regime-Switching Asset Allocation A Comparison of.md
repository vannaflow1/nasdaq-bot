# Mitigating Sequence-of-Returns Risk with Regime-Switching Asset Allocation: A Comparison of Econometric and Deep Learning Models in Portfolio Decumulation
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-11243482/latest.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The paper finds that regime‑switching asset allocation can dramatically cut retirement‑portfolio ruin rates at modest withdrawal rates (e.g., 4% withdrawal reduces depletion from 3.30% to about 0.6% using a Markov‑switching model or an LSTM), but at higher withdrawal rates the same filters can hurt performance. Deep‑learning (LSTM) does not consistently identify regimes better than econometric models, yet it keeps more capital by staying invested rather than by timing.

## Why it matters (or not)
The focus is on retirement decumulation rather than active trading of Nasdaq/S&P futures or options.

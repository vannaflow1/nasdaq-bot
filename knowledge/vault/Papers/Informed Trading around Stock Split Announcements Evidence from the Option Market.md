# Informed Trading around Stock Split Announcements: Evidence from the Option Market
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/82E163122F360CD32299EAF002B4B6AC/S0022109017000023a.pdf/div-class-title-informed-trading-around-stock-split-announcements-evidence-from-the-option-market-div.pdf)

## What Volta learned
Looking at option implied volatility around stock-split announcements, the authors find that option traders correctly expect a stock to get more volatile after it splits. There is only weak evidence that options traders anticipate the positive price drift splitting firms tend to show. The option measures help predict post-split volatility levels and changes, but not returns.

## Why it matters (or not)
A single-stock corporate-event study; a split in a big Nasdaq name barely moves QQQ, and we have no per-stock event data to test it.

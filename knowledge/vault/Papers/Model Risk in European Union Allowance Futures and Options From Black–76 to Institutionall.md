# Model Risk in European Union Allowance Futures and Options: From Black–76 to Institutionally Grounded Models—A Critical Structured Review
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://journals.zeuspress.org/index.php/conference/article/download/1555/1493)

## What Volta learned
The paper reviews how pricing models for EU emissions allowance futures and options have moved from simple Black‑76 to more complex models that try to embed regulatory features like the Market Stability Reserve. It argues that the simplest model that matches the current regime and has empirical support should be used, and that many of the more sophisticated models add identification and numerical risks.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study focuses on EU emissions trading, not on Nasdaq/S&P futures or options, so it offers little actionable insight for typical retail traders.

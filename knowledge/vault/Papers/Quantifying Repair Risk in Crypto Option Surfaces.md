# Quantifying Repair Risk in Crypto Option Surfaces
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.1145/3858063.3858699)

## What Volta learned
When building risk models for crypto options, the way you smooth or adjust raw option quotes into an arbitrage‑free surface (the "repair") can cause large differences in Greeks and hedge sizes even if the adjusted prices are very close.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper deals with crypto option surface construction and shows variability in Greeks, which has little direct application to retail trading of Nasdaq/S&P futures or options.

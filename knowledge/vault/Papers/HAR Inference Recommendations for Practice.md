# HAR Inference: Recommendations for Practice
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10160486/1/har_JBES_v8.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The paper discusses methods for improving heteroscedasticity- and autocorrelation-robust (HAR) inference in time series regression. It recommends using a larger-than-usual truncation parameter to estimate the long-run variance, combined with fixed-b critical values, to reduce size distortions.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper provides technical recommendations for improving statistical inference in time series regression, which may be useful for traders or quants working with financial time series data, but it does not directly address trading strategies or market behavior.

## What Volta learned
The paper compares minimum‑variance and CVaR‑based portfolio optimization for equity‑bond ETFs (2020‑2024), finding lower VaR/CVaR but not higher risk‑adjusted returns versus an equal‑weight benchmark; GARCH outperforms FIGARCH for volatility modeling, and a Normal Inverse Gaussian model is used to generate option prices.

## Why it matters (or not)
Provides useful insights on tail‑risk‑focused optimization and volatility modeling that can inform a trader’s risk‑management toolbox.

# A review of machine learning experiments in equity investment decision-making: why most published research findings do not live up to their promise in real life
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s41060-021-00245-5.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The paper reviews 27 academic experiments on machine learning in equity investment decision-making, finding a gap between published research claims and real-life performance. Most research findings do not translate to profitable investment strategies in real life. The authors criticize 'cherry-picking' of best-performing models and offer recommendations for future experiments.

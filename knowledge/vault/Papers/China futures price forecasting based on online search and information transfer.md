# China futures price forecasting based on online search and information transfer
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666764922000376/pdf)

## What Volta learned
The paper proposes using combined Baidu and Google search volume data (via PCA) and transfer entropy to feed a GWO‑optimized CNN‑LSTM model for daily price forecasts of Chinese commodity futures.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study targets Chinese agricultural futures and relies on search data not available for US Nasdaq/S&P products.

## Concepts
online search metrics, transfer entropy, cnn‑lstm forecasting, hybrid optimization

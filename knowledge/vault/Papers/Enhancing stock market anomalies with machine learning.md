# Enhancing stock market anomalies with machine learning
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11156-022-01099-z.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The study examines the use of machine learning to enhance stock market anomalies, finding that machine learning models can generate significant monthly returns of 1.8-2.0%. Over 80% of the models outperformed a linearly constructed baseline factor. The results suggest that non-linear models can reveal market inefficiencies that are hard to explain by risk-based factors.

## Why it matters (or not)
The abstract is related to stock market anomalies and machine learning, but it does not directly discuss trading strategies, risk management, or volatility models.

# Black–Scholes Pricing and Market Mispricing of XSP European Index Options
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://aemps.ewapub.com/article/view/37764.pdf)

## What Volta learned
On a single day, Black‑Scholes using a common 20‑day historical volatility over‑prices XSP calls (especially near‑the‑money) and under‑prices puts, with mean absolute errors up to 1.43 for ATM calls and ARPE around 56% for calls versus 60% for puts.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper quantifies systematic mispricing patterns that can inform simple option‑selection rules.

## Concepts
black‑scholes pricing error, moneyness bias, atm call overpricing, historical volatility input, relative pricing error

## Idea
Short (sell) near‑ATM XSP calls that have a high absolute relative pricing error (e.g., ARPE > 30%) and buy the corresponding puts of similar moneyness, betting that the market will correct the call overprice while the put is fairly priced.

# Volume-Synchronized Probability of Informed Trading (VPIN), Market Volatility, and High-Frequency Liquidity
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](http://hdl.handle.net/10464/6711)

## What Volta learned
The study examines the relationship between Volume-Synchronized Probability of Informed Trading (VPIN) and market volatility, as well as high-frequency liquidity. It finds that VPIN has a positive association with market volatility and a feedback effect with liquidity, where a negative liquidity shock can boost VPIN and lead to further liquidity drain.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study provides useful insights into market microstructure and volatility, but its direct application to trading strategies for retail traders is limited.

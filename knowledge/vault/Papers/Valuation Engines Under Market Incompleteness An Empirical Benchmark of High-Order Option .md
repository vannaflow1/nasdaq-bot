# Valuation Engines Under Market Incompleteness: An Empirical Benchmark of High-Order Option Pricing in Digital Asset Markets
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://journals.zeuspress.org/index.php/conference/article/download/1525/1466)

## What Volta learned
The paper shows that for crypto options, the Black‑Scholes model is far off; using Heston cuts pricing RMSE by ~45% and a stochastic‑volatility‑with‑jumps model improves deep OTM pricing errors by >70%. It argues that jumps and stochastic volatility are essential in incomplete markets.

## Why it matters (or not)
Focuses on crypto options, not Nasdaq/S&P futures, and provides no directly usable numbers for our dataset.

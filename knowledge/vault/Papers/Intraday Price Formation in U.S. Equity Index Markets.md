# Intraday Price Formation in U.S. Equity Index Markets
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1046/j.1540-6261.2003.00609.x)

## What Volta learned
Hasbrouck studied where prices are actually discovered among the instruments that track U.S. stock indexes: floor-traded futures, E-mini futures, ETFs and sector ETFs. For both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq-100, most price discovery happens in the E-mini futures, meaning futures tend to move first and ETFs like QQQ follow. For the S&P 400 MidCap it is shared between the regular futures and the ETF, and SPY leads the sector ETFs far more than they lead it. Worked from the abstract only (no full text in the repo), so no numeric shares are given.

## Why it matters (or not)
Confirms that NQ futures, not QQQ, lead Nasdaq-100 price moves intraday, which supports anchoring levels on NQ, but it is an intraday lead-lag result that Volta's daily memory cannot test.

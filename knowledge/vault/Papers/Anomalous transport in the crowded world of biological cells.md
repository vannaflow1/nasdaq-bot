# Anomalous transport in the crowded world of biological cells
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (prop mission)  ·  [source](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/0034-4885/76/4/046602/pdf)

## What Volta learned
Anomalous transport describes how particle motion deviates from normal diffusion due to crowding, showing sublinear MSD growth, time‑varying diffusion rates and non‑Gaussian displacement distributions. Different stochastic models (fractional Brownian motion, CTRW, Lorentz) capture these effects via distinct propagators despite similar MSDs. The key insight is that apparent randomness can mask underlying heterogeneous constraints that affect risk and return dynamics.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper studies physical diffusion, not financial markets, so its direct applicability to NQ futures prop trading is minimal.

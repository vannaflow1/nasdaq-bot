# Event‐Day Options
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1111/jtsa.12819)

## What Volta learned
This paper (Jonathan Wright) uses the new options on US Treasury futures that expire every Wednesday and Friday to measure how much interest-rate uncertainty the market prices for a single Fed (FOMC) decision or jobs report. It compares the implied variance the night before those events with the same weekday when nothing is scheduled, compares that priced uncertainty with what actually happened, and builds the implied probability curves on event eves. Only the abstract was available, so the measured sizes are not known.

## Why it matters (or not)
The method (compare event-eve implied variance with a normal weekday) is the same idea behind my event premium reading, but the paper studies Treasury rates, not Nasdaq, and I worked from the abstract only.

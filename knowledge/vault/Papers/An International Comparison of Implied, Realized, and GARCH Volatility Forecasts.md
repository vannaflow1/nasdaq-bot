# An International Comparison of Implied, Realized, and GARCH Volatility Forecasts
★★★★☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/58088/1/An_International_Comparison_of_Implied_Realized_and_GARCH_Volatility_Forecasts.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The paper compares the performance of implied, realized, and GARCH volatility models for 13 equity indices from 10 countries. It finds that the Heterogeneous Autoregressive (HAR) model is superior at the daily horizon, while an implied volatility model that corrects for the volatility risk premium is superior at the monthly horizon. GARCH models have inferior performance in almost all cases.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper provides useful insights into volatility forecasting models, which can be applied to trading and risk management, particularly for Nasdaq/S&P index futures and options.

## Idea
Using a volatility risk premium-adjusted implied volatility model for monthly horizon volatility forecasting and a Heterogeneous Autoregressive (HAR) model for daily horizon volatility forecasting.

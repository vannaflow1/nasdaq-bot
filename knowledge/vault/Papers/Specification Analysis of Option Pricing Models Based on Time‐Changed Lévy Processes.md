# Specification Analysis of Option Pricing Models Based on Time‐Changed Lévy Processes
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2004.00667.x)

## What Volta learned
The authors (Huang and Wu) compare many option-pricing models for S&P 500 index options that are built by speeding up or slowing down a jump process's clock. The best fit needs frequent small jumps in returns and two separate sources of changing volatility, one tied to the jumps and one to the smooth part of price moves. Worked from the abstract only (no full text in the repo), so no fit statistics are given.

## Why it matters (or not)
A technical model-comparison study for pricing index options; it gives no trading signal or rule for NQ/QQQ day-trading decisions.

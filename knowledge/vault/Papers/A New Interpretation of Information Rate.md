# A New Interpretation of Information Rate
★☆☆☆☆  ·  Must-read (curated)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1956.tb03809.x)

## What Volta learned
The abstract discusses a new interpretation of information rate in a communication channel, relating it to a gambler's ability to grow their capital exponentially using received symbols and fair odds. This growth rate equals the information transmission rate over the channel. The concept is also generalized for arbitrary odds.

## Why it matters (or not)
The abstract seems to focus on a theoretical concept in information theory and its relation to gambling, without direct application to trading or quant models.

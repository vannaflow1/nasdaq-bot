# Option Return Predictability
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://utoronto.scholaris.ca/bitstreams/5c530340-f282-4a35-a281-76336e3757b1/download)

## What Volta learned
For single-stock options, the return to writing delta-hedged calls can be predicted from company traits: it is lower for high-priced, more profitable firms and higher for firms with lots of cash, volatile cash flows, new share issuance, distress risk and analysts who disagree. Long-short option portfolios built this way had annual Sharpe ratios above two and stayed profitable after trading costs. Two option-specific factors explain the profits; ordinary stock risk factors do not.

## Why it matters (or not)
A cross-sectional single-stock options effect based on company fundamentals; it does not apply to an index like QQQ/NDX and our memory has no firm-level data.

## Concepts
delta-hedged option returns, [[cross-section]], firm characteristics, option factors

# Predictive Value of Commercial Trading Activity in Eurodollar Futures Market on US Stock Market Indices From 2000 Through 2019
★★☆☆☆  ·  Hunt: overnight trading range macroeconomic announcements Nasdaq i  ·  [source](https://hdl.handle.net/2027.42/155357)

## What Volta learned
The paper reproduces a strong (≈0.8) correlation when shifting monthly commercial Eurodollar futures positioning forward by 52 weeks against the S&P 500, but most statistical tests (cointegration, Granger‑causality) find the positioning not a significant predictor for the S&P and other US equity indices from 2000‑2019.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study links a macro futures market to equity indices but shows limited predictive power, offering little actionable insight for traders.

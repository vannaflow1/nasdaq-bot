# Buzzwords build momentum: Global financial Twitter sentiment and the aggregate stock market
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0957417419304270/pdf)

## What Volta learned
The study examines the relationship between Twitter sentiment and stock market developments in five major economic regions. Expert Twitter users, whose tweets focus on finance, drive the interdependence between Twitter sentiment and financial markets. Their sentiment metrics can predict stock index returns, especially during times of negative market returns.

## Why it matters (or not)
The abstract suggests a relationship between Twitter sentiment and stock market returns, but its direct application to trading is limited.

## Concepts
twitter sentiment, expert users, stock index returns, momentum strategies

## Idea

## What Volta learned
ETFs, especially leveraged ones, push VIX futures prices up by about 18.5% per year through rebalancing trades, and betting against that rebalancing has yielded Sharpe > 2.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper quantifies an ETF‑driven premium in VIX futures, which is directly relevant to futures/option traders but does not cover S&P/NASDAQ specifics.

## Concepts
etf leverage rebalancing, vix futures pricing bias, short‑both strategy, non‑fundamental price component

## Idea
When VIX futures term structure is steep (high term ratio) and the daily ETF net asset change is positive (using the ETF AUM series if available), go short the front‑month VIX future and long the next‑month future (a short‑both spread) to capture the leverage‑rebalancing premium.

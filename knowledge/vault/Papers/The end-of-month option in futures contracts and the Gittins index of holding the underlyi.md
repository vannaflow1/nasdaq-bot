# The end-of-month option in futures contracts and the Gittins index of holding the underlying
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.21196)

## What Volta learned
The paper discusses the end-of-month option in futures contracts, which allows the short to choose when to deliver during a window with a fixed delivery price. The authors characterize optimal delivery through a universal index obtained from the Bank-El Karoui representation theorem.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper provides useful knowledge on optimal stopping and futures pricing, but its direct application to trading is limited.

gittins index, optimal stopping, futures pricing, stochastic convenience yield

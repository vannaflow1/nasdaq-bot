# Deep learning for financial forecasting: A review of recent trends
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.iref.2025.104719)

## What Volta learned
The paper reviews 187 studies on deep learning applications for financial forecasting from 2020-2024, focusing on architectures, forecasting tasks, and model types. It finds that RNN-based models, particularly LSTMs, dominate the field, while hybrid architectures combining convolutional and recurrent components are increasingly used. The review highlights the need for more robust models under extreme market conditions and standardized evaluation protocols.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper provides a comprehensive review of deep learning applications in financial forecasting, but does not present specific testable trading ideas or concrete numbers.

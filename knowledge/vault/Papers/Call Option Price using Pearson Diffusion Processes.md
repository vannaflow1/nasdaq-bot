# Call Option Price using Pearson Diffusion Processes
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.14577)

## What Volta learned
The paper proposes pricing European call options by modeling log‑returns with Pearson diffusion processes, whose stationary distribution (Pearson Type IV) can capture skewness and excess kurtosis; they prove risk‑neutral measure validity and claim better pricing performance versus Black‑Scholes and Heston on Nifty 50 options.

## Why it matters (or not)
Offers a novel non‑Gaussian diffusion model for option pricing, but no concrete empirical results for US indices or actionable metrics for traders.

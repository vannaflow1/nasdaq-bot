# Neural Network–Based Financial Volatility Forecasting: A Systematic Review
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3483596)

## What Volta learned
This paper reviews 35 studies on using neural networks for financial volatility forecasting, published after 2015. It highlights issues with comparing models and a gap between modern machine learning and those used in volatility forecasting. The authors propose a shared task to evaluate models and suggest ways to bridge the gap.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper is a review of existing research on neural network-based volatility forecasting, with no specific trading strategy or results.

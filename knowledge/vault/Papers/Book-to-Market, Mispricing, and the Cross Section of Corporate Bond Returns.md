# Book-to-Market, Mispricing, and the Cross Section of Corporate Bond Returns
☆☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/9F30CC8EE3077365BDF05E801333CB26/S0022109024000048a.pdf/div-class-title-book-to-market-mispricing-and-the-cross-section-of-corporate-bond-returns-div.pdf)

## What Volta learned
Corporate bonds whose book value is high relative to their market price tend to earn more later: senior bonds, even investment grade, in the top 20% by this ratio beat the bottom 20% by about 3-4% a year after controlling for yield, spread, rating, duration, liquidity and past returns. The edge fades when trading is delayed and appears even where risk differences are small, which the authors read as mispricing rather than compensation for risk.

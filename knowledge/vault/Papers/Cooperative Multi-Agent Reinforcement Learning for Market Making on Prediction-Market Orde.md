# Cooperative Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for Market Making on Prediction-Market Order Books
★★★☆☆  ·  arXiv  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26789v1)

## What Volta learned
This paper studies market makers trained with multi-agent reinforcement learning on prediction markets, specifically Polymarket. The authors address challenges such as adverse selection, delayed consequences of quotes, and limited data, and propose a cooperative multi-agent decomposition into a quoter and a risk manager.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper is related to market making and reinforcement learning, but its focus on prediction markets and thin order books makes it less directly applicable to Nasdaq/S&P index futures and options trading.

## Concepts
multi-agent reinforcement learning, [[market making]], prediction markets

## Idea
Can a cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning approach improve market making performance in thin and volatile markets?

# Global Uncertainty and BRICS+ Equity Markets: Spillovers from VIX, Geopolitical Risk, and U.S. Macro-Financial Shocks
★★☆☆☆  ·  Frontier (rising, < 18 months old)  ·  [source](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-9091/13/11/217/pdf?version=1762235577)

## What Volta learned
The study uses a VAR model (2016‑2025) to show that U.S. VIX, geopolitical risk, inflation expectations, and the term spread cause short‑run spillovers to emerging‑market equities (BRICS+), with VIX acting both as a source and a sink of volatility. Effects fade within a few days, suggesting rapid market adjustment.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper focuses on emerging‑market equities rather than Nasdaq/S&P futures or options, offering limited direct trading insight.

# The Control Layer Behind Rule-Following Agents Trading Across Market Conditions
★★★☆☆  ·  arXiv  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26324v1)

## What Volta learned
This study tested four AI trading agents to see if they followed their strategies and risk rules. The agents were analyzed over 2,627 closed positions from February 12 to August 27, 2026, with a 0.09% round-trip fee. The agents showed high rule adherence, with 97.5% of gross loss within declared stops.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper is about testing AI trading agents for rule adherence, which is relevant to traders and quants but does not provide direct trading strategies or market analysis.

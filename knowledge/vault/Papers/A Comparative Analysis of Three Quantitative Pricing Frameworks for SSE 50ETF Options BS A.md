# A Comparative Analysis of Three Quantitative Pricing Frameworks for SSE 50ETF Options: BS Analytical Model, CRR Binomial Tree Model and Monte Carlo Simulation
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://aemps.ewapub.com/article/view/39090.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The paper shows that Black‑Scholes, a 200‑step CRR binomial tree, and a Monte‑Carlo simulation with 100 k paths produce virtually identical prices for a European call on China's SSE 50ETF (≈0.0845). It highlights the trade‑off: BS is fastest but limited to European options; CRR can handle American features with more compute; Monte‑Carlo handles high‑dimensional or path‑dependent payoffs but needs many paths.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study concerns Chinese ETF options and basic pricing methods, offering little new insight for trading US/NASDAQ futures or options.

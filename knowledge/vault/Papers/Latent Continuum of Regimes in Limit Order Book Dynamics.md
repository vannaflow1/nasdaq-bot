# Latent Continuum of Regimes in Limit Order Book Dynamics
★★☆☆☆  ·  Frontier (arXiv, last 45 days)  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05740v2)

## What Volta learned
The paper finds that high-frequency limit order book dynamics are better described by a continuous low‑dimensional manifold (effective dimension ~1.4) rather than a few discrete regimes, with a single latent coordinate explaining >83% of the variation and tracking market stress.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study uses Euro‑Stoxx futures LOB data and advanced geometry, offering conceptual insight but no directly applicable models or numbers for Nasdaq/Q‑QQ futures trading.

## Concepts
continuous regime space, latent stress coordinate, riemannian covariance geometry, low‑dimensional market stress, walk‑forward forecasting

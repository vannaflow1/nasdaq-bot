# Alternative data and sentiment analysis: Prospecting non-standard data in machine learning-driven finance
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.1177/20539517211070701)

## What Volta learned
The paper discusses 'alternative data' in finance, which includes non-standard data sources like social media commentary, satellite imagery, and GPS data. These data are used for stock price prediction, risk exposure detection, and discovering new price movement indicators. Machine learning analytics tools are making alternative data more useful in investment management and algorithmic trading.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper is more focused on the concept and implications of alternative data rather than providing specific trading strategies or models.

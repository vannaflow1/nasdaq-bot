# Cross‐Market Convergence Trading in Options Order Flow
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1002/fut.70142)

## What Volta learned
The authors separate options order flow driven by arbitrage-style 'convergence' trading (using matched calls and puts to build synthetic stock or futures positions when options are mispriced against the futures or spot market) from flow driven by traders with private information. This convergence flow grows as the options-futures mispricing widens and predicts returns in the related market in the opposite direction, unlike informed flow. Foreign investors mostly respond to options-versus-futures mispricing and domestic investors to options-versus-spot mispricing, and traders react more to spot underpricing than overpricing, consistent with limits on short selling.

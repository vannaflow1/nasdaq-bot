# Statistical Modelling of Stock-Market Volatility Forecasting with Investor Sentiment
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://ijfi.net/index.php/ijfi/article/download/140/137)

## What Volta learned
The paper shows how to improve daily volatility forecasts by adding a retail‑investor sentiment index (built from search volume and market‑behavior proxies) into GARCH‑type models, especially using a mixed‑frequency GARCH‑MIDAS framework, and compares it to an LSTM benchmark.

## Why it matters (or not)
It focuses on Chinese equities and sentiment data not directly available for US Nasdaq futures, offering limited practical use for a trader.

## Concepts
garch‑midas, sentiment index, mixed‑frequency regression, out‑of‑sample forecast evaluation, behavioral volatility

# No-Regret Revenue Maximization in Sequential Market Making Under Inventory Constraints
★★☆☆☆  ·  Frontier (arXiv, last 45 days)  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24562v1)

## What Volta learned
The paper designs online algorithms for a market maker that posts bid and ask prices to unknown buyer/seller valuations while keeping its inventory small. With only trade‑success feedback, regret grows as T^{2/3}; with limit‑order‑book feedback (seeing the counter‑party’s valuation when a trade fails), regret improves to √T. It also shows a lower bound linking tighter per‑round inventory limits to higher unavoidable regret.

## Why it matters (or not)
The results are about optimal market‑making algorithms rather than retail trading or futures/options strategies, so they offer limited practical insight for most traders.

## Concepts
online learning, regret minimization, inventory constraints, limit order book feedback, dynamic bid‑ask quoting

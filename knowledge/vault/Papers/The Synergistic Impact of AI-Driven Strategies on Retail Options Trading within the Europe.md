# The Synergistic Impact of AI-Driven Strategies on Retail Options Trading within the European Derivatives Market: A Comparative Analysis of Liquidity and Market Efficiency
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.51483/ijaiml.5.2.2025.1-15)

## What Volta learned
This descriptive study compares US and European index options and argues that AI trading tools work better where markets are deeper: SPX trades about 1.2 million contracts a day versus about 300,000 for Euro Stoxx 50, with spreads of roughly 0.25% versus 0.40%. It cites options volume rising 18% in the US versus 9% in Europe after CPI releases and claims AI reduces retail behavioral biases, but this rests largely on industry reports rather than a tested model.

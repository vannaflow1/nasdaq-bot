# Intraday time‐series momentum: Evidence from China
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.3493927)

## What Volta learned
Intraday time-series momentum exists in Chinese commodity futures, where the first half-hour return predicts the last half-hour return. This momentum is stronger in high-volume or high-volatility trading sessions.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study focuses on Chinese commodity futures, which may not directly apply to Nasdaq/S&P index futures and options, but the concept of intraday momentum is still relevant.

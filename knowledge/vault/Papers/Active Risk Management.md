# Active Risk Management

## What Volta learned
The paper distinguishes passive market beta from active alpha, explains alpha–beta separation, return attribution (e.g., Brinson), and how tracking error measures active risk relative to a benchmark. It also discusses benchmark design, active risk budgeting, and differences between public and private markets.

## Why it matters (or not)
The abstract is about institutional portfolio risk management with no direct models or data for trading futures/options.

## Concepts
alpha-beta separation, tracking error, return attribution, benchmark design, active risk budgeting

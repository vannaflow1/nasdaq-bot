# Inferring Dealer Networks in the Foreign Exchange Market Using Conditional Transfer Entropy: Analysis of a Central Bank Announcement

## What Volta learned
The paper proposes using conditional transfer entropy on high‑frequency dealer bid/ask data to map how information from events (e.g., an ECB announcement) flows through FX dealer networks, identifying dealers that act as sources, conduits, or sinks of information.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study focuses on FX dealer networks and does not provide models or data directly applicable to Nasdaq/S&P futures or options trading.

# Autoregressive Conditional Heteroscedasticity with Estimates of the Variance of United Kingdom Inflation
★★★★☆  ·  Must-read (curated)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.2307/1912773)

## What Volta learned
The paper introduces the concept of Autoregressive Conditional Heteroscedasticity (ARCH) processes, which model time-varying variances in financial data. It presents a new approach to estimate variance, showing that recent past data can inform future variance forecasts. The ARCH model is applied to UK inflation data, revealing significant ARCH effects and increased variances during the 1970s.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper is highly relevant as it introduces the ARCH model, which is widely used in finance to model volatility, a crucial aspect for traders and quants.

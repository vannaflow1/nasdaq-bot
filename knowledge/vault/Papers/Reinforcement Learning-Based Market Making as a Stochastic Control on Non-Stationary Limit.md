# Reinforcement Learning-Based Market Making as a Stochastic Control on Non-Stationary Limit Order Book Dynamics
★★☆☆☆  ·  Frontier (rising, < 18 months old)  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.12456)

## What Volta learned
The paper shows how to train a market‑making agent with Proximal Policy Optimization on a simulated limit‑order‑book that mimics real‑world features like clustered order arrivals, time‑varying spreads, and inventory risk, and that the resulting RL policy can outperform the classic Avellaneda‑Stoikov solution in non‑stationary regimes.

## Why it matters (or not)
Primarily about high‑frequency market making; limited direct use for daily futures/options trading.

# Option Volume and Stock Prices: Evidence on Where Informed Traders Trade
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1111/0022-1082.194060)

## What Volta learned
This classic paper (Easley, O'Hara and Srinivas) models informed traders choosing between the stock and its options, and shows when they prefer options, for example for the leverage. Testing with intraday option data, the authors find that option volume split into bullish ('positive', such as call buys and put sells) and bearish ('negative') trades carries information about where the stock price goes next. The abstract gives no specific numbers.

## Why it matters (or not)
Worked from the abstract only (no full text available); the idea that signed options flow leads price is relevant to QQQ, but Volta's memory has no signed option volume, so it cannot be tested yet.

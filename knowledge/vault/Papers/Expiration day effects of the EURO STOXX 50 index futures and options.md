# Expiration day effects of the EURO STOXX 50 index futures and options
★☆☆☆☆  ·  Hunt: Asian session daily return reversal Nasdaq index futures  ·  [source](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/handle/123456789/429)

## What Volta learned
The study finds that on the monthly options and quarterly futures expiration days of the EURO STOXX 50, trading volume and intraday volatility (especially around 12:00 CET) are unusually high, and there is a pattern of higher returns before expiration and lower returns after, leading to a reversal effect.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper examines European index futures/options, not Nasdaq/S&P, so little direct use for US index traders.

## Concepts
expiration day effect, intraday volatility spike, return reversal, program trading, liquidity driven moves

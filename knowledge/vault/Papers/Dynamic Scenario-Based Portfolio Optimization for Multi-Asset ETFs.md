# Dynamic Scenario-Based Portfolio Optimization for Multi-Asset ETFs
★★☆☆☆  ·  arXiv  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16540v1)

## What Volta learned
The paper tests whether using ARMA‑GARCH marginal models plus a Student‑t copula to generate conditional return scenarios improves multi‑ETF portfolio performance versus simply using historical rolling‑window returns. Across 946 daily rebalancing periods, the scenario‑based method did not consistently deliver higher risk‑adjusted returns or lower downside risk, and results were sensitive to trading constraints.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study is about multi‑ETF portfolio construction, not directly about Nasdaq/S&P futures or options, and offers no actionable pricing or signal model.

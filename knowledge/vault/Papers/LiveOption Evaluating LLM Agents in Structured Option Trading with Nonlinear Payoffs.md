# LiveOption: Evaluating LLM Agents in Structured Option Trading with Nonlinear Payoffs
★★☆☆☆  ·  Frontier (arXiv, last 45 days)  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33470v1)

## What Volta learned
The paper introduces LiveOption, a benchmark that tests large‑language‑model agents on realistic option‑trading tasks (portfolio overlays, earnings‑driven bets, and 0‑day‑to‑expiry intraday trading) with full execution, margin, and risk constraints, and shows that current LLM agents underperform.

## Why it matters (or not)
The work is about evaluating AI agents, not providing new models or signals for traders, so it offers limited direct trading insight.

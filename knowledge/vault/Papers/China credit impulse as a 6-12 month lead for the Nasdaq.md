# China credit impulse as a 6-12 month lead for the Nasdaq
★★★☆☆  ·  owner tip  ·  [source](https://en.macromicro.me/charts/35559/china-credit-impulse-index)

## What Volta learned
Credit impulse = change in China's 12-month credit flow vs a year earlier, as % of credit. Claimed to lead risk assets by 6-12 months.

## Why it matters (or not)
monthly/quarterly macro; at best a long/short regime tilt, no daily signal

## Concepts
credit impulse, china, macro regime

## Idea
BIS quarterly China credit impulse (3-month publication lag) vs next 3-12 months of NDX

## Test
TESTED 2000-2024: corr with the next 6m NDX +0.15, next 12m +0.20 (positive in 2000-09, 2010-17, 2018-26), but only ~25-50 independent periods -> not significant. A 'long while the impulse rises' rule did WORSE than buy-and-hold. BIS data ends 2024-12; monthly TSF data would be needed live.

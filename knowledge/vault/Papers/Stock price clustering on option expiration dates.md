# Stock price clustering on option expiration dates
★★★☆☆  ·  Must-read (curated)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2004.08.005)

## What Volta learned
The paper explores stock price clustering on option expiration dates, a phenomenon where stock prices tend to cluster at certain levels, such as round numbers or strike prices, particularly on days when options expire. This clustering can be attributed to various factors, including market microstructure, investor behavior, and the impact of option trading on underlying stock prices.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper's findings can provide insights into market behavior around option expiration dates, which may be useful for traders, but it does not directly provide a testable trading strategy or model.

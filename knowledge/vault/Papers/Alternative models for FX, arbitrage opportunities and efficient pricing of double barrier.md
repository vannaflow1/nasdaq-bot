# Alternative models for FX, arbitrage opportunities and efficient pricing of double barrier options in regime-switching Lévy models
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.03915)

## What Volta learned
The paper shows that double‑no‑touch (DNT) option prices can differ dramatically between diffusion (Heston) and pure‑jump (KoBoL) models, especially when barriers are close, and proposes a fast numerical method (GWR‑SINH) for pricing such options in Lévy and regime‑switching models.

## Why it matters (or not)
Provides insight into potential DNT mis‑pricing and a fast pricing algorithm, but no direct empirical results for Nasdaq futures in the paper.

## Concepts
double‑no‑touch options, pure‑jump lévy models, regime‑switching, gaver‑wynn‑rho acceleration, model mis‑pricing arbitrage

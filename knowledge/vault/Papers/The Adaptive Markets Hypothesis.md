# The Adaptive Markets Hypothesis
★★★☆☆  ·  Must-read (curated)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2004.442611)

## What Volta learned
The Adaptive Markets Hypothesis (AMH) reconciles the Efficient Markets Hypothesis (EMH) with behavioral finance by applying evolutionary principles to financial interactions. It suggests that market participants adapt to changing environments using simple heuristics, which can lead to seemingly irrational behavior. This perspective offers implications for portfolio management.

## Why it matters (or not)
The abstract provides a theoretical framework that might influence a trader's understanding of market behavior, but concrete trading strategies or testable ideas are not provided.

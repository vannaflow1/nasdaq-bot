# Volatility Information Trading in the Option Market
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2008.01352.x)

## What Volta learned
This Journal of Finance paper built a measure of how much volatility non-market-maker traders were buying or selling, from the trading volume of individual stock options, and found that this net volatility demand predicted the stocks' later realized volatility. Buying volatility also pushed option prices up, and that price impact grew by about 40% in the days before earnings announcements, when some traders may know more about the coming move, then fell back to normal once the news was out. In short, option order flow can carry information about future volatility, especially ahead of scheduled events.

## Why it matters (or not)
The idea that net option buying of volatility foreshadows bigger moves around scheduled events is relevant to NQ/QQQ options, but the evidence is from single-stock options around earnings and I worked from the abstract only because no full text was available.

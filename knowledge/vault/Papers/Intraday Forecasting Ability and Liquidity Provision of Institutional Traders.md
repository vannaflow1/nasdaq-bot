# Intraday Forecasting Ability and Liquidity Provision of Institutional Traders
★★☆☆☆  ·  arXiv  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31141v1)

## What Volta learned
The paper finds that institutional traders with measurable short‑term price‑forecasting skill generate higher intraday profits, and that providing liquidity (passive executions) adds a sizable profit boost.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study is about stock‑level high‑frequency trading, not directly applicable to Nasdaq futures/options or the provided volatility‑related columns.

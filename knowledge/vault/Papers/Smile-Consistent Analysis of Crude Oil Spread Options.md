# Smile-Consistent Analysis of Crude Oil Spread Options
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.3390/risks14110261)

## What Volta learned
The paper tests a theoretical relation between ATM spread implied volatility and the underlying futures' volatilities & correlation, extending it to the ATM spread skew using local volatility models on crude oil spread options.

## Why it matters (or not)
Focuses on crude oil spread options, with no direct implications for equity index futures or common retail strategies.

atm spread volatility, spread skew, local volatility model, crude oil calendar spreads

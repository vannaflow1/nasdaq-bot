# Rachev-Ratio Portfolio Optimization with TimeGAN Scenarios: Replacement versus Supplementation
★★★☆☆  ·  arXiv  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24544v1)

## What Volta learned
This paper studies the use of TimeGAN-generated scenarios in Rachev-ratio portfolio optimization. The study finds that generated scenarios can understate volatility and tail losses, and distort dependence. The results are mixed, with higher-mass hybrids earning larger average net returns in some configurations, but not consistently.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper explores the use of generated scenarios in portfolio optimization, which is relevant to traders and quants, but the focus on Rachev-ratio optimization and TimeGAN scenarios limits its direct applicability.

## Concepts
timegan, rachev ratio, portfolio optimization, scenario generation, [[tail risk]]

## Idea
Can a hybrid approach combining historical and generated scenarios improve portfolio performance in a Rachev-ratio optimization framework?

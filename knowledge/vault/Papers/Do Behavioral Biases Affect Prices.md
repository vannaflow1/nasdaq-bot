# Do Behavioral Biases Affect Prices?
★★★★☆  ·  OpenAlex (prop mission)  ·  [source](http://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/bitstream/2027.42/115930/1/jofi723.pdf)

## What Volta learned
CBOT proprietary traders show loss aversion: after morning losses they take excess afternoon risk, buying high and selling low, causing temporary price distortion that is quickly corrected.

## Why it matters (or not)
Understanding when prices are likely being moved by loss‑averse traders gives Volta a short‑term edge to trade against the distortion.

## Concepts
loss aversion, afternoon risk taking, price reversal, temporary mispricing, behavioral price impact

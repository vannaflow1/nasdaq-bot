# Can Large Language Models Beat Wall Street? Unveiling the Potential of AI in Stock Selection
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.03737)

## What Volta learned
MarketSenseAI uses GPT-4 to read news, fundamentals, price trends and macro data and issue buy/hold/sell calls on S&P 100 stocks with written explanations. Over a 15-month test it reported excess returns of 10% to 30% and a cumulative return of up to 72%, with risk similar to the market. The test is short and covers a single period, and the paper is by the framework's own developers.

## Why it matters (or not)
Read the repo's full text; it concerns monthly single-stock picking over a short 15-month window, not NQ/QQQ options or next-day index moves.

## Concepts
large language models, gpt-4 stock selection, chain-of-thought, news and fundamentals analysis, explainable signals

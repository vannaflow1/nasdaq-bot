# Do Options-Flow Signatures of Dealer Hedging Predict Price Direction Around Earnings?
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://research-archive.org/index.php/rars/preprint/download/4297/5900/5450)

## What Volta learned
The paper tests four options‑flow metrics (volatility smirk, delta difference, open‑interest ratio, option‑volume ratio) combined into a weighted “Agg Score” to see if dealer hedging around earnings predicts next‑day price direction. Across 30 earnings events the composite hit rate was 48.2%, no better than random or a simple momentum baseline, indicating little directional power but suggesting dealer hedging may affect volatility more.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study shows these flow signals don’t help predict next‑day direction, so a trader would likely ignore them for directional bets.

## Concepts
options flow, [[dealer hedging]], [[volatility smirk]], delta difference, earnings surprise

## Idea
Use the four flow indicators (especially volatility smirk) as a predictor of next‑day implied volatility spike rather than price direction around earnings.

# LOB-Impact Benchmark: Emergent Market Impact in Generative Limit Order Book Models
★★☆☆☆  ·  arXiv  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27100v1)

## What Volta learned
Neural limit order book generators, trained just to predict the next event, naturally exhibit market impact when simulated metaorders are injected, but the observed impact exponents (0.63‑1.21) are higher than the classic square‑root law (0.5) and they show little decay after execution.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper studies simulation fidelity rather than direct trading strategies, offering limited actionable insight for retail traders.

generative lob models, market impact exponent, metaorder injection, impact decay, benchmark evaluation

# Journal of Economic Surveys

## What Volta learned
Despite its catalogue title, this entry is a survey of interest-rate models (from the Journal of Economic Surveys) that organises the field into five layers: building the yield curve, arbitrage-free rate dynamics, representing the volatility smile, collateral and post-LIBOR risk-free-rate conventions, and numerical implementation. It walks through the history from Vasicek and Cox-Ingersoll-Ross to Hull-White, HJM, the LIBOR market model, SABR and post-LIBOR forward market models, arguing each arose to handle new products and market practices rather than one model 'winning'. It warns that fitting today's vanilla option prices exactly can still hide risks in correlation, future smile behaviour and numerical error; the abstract gives no empirical numbers.

## Why it matters (or not)
Not relevant to NQ/QQQ options or day-trading: it is a methods survey for pricing interest-rate derivatives; worked from the abstract only because no full text was available.

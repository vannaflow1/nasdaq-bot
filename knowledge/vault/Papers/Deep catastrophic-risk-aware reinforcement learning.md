# Deep catastrophic-risk-aware reinforcement learning
★☆☆☆☆  ·  arXiv  ·  [source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27214v1)

## What Volta learned
The paper shows how to combine Extreme Value Theory with deep reinforcement learning to better estimate and minimize tail risk (CVaR) when extreme loss events are rare, using a differentiable loss based on a Generalized Pareto Distribution.

## Why it matters (or not)
The work is methodological and does not provide concrete trading rules or results for Nasdaq/S&P futures, so it offers little actionable insight for retail traders.

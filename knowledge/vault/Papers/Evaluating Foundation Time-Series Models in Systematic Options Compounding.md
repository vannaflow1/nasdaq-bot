## What Volta learned
The paper finds that zero‑shot large language time‑series models (TimesFM) and physics‑inspired kinematic rules add no meaningful edge to systematic options‑selling strategies on AMZN, GOOG, and NVDA when volatility premium is stripped out, and can even hurt performance out‑of‑sample.

## Why it matters (or not)
Examines options selling on individual equities using AI forecasts, but offers no actionable insight for Nasdaq/S&P futures or broader trading.

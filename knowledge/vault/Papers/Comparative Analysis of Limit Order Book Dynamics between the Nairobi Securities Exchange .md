# Comparative Analysis of Limit Order Book Dynamics between the Nairobi Securities Exchange and Major Global Exchanges: Insights into Liquidity, Volatility, and Market Efficiency
★☆☆☆☆  ·  Hunt: Volume Synchronized Probability of Informed Trading VPIN Mar  ·  [source](https://journals.eanso.org/index.php/ijfa/article/download/3500/4055)

## What Volta learned
The paper finds that Nairobi’s limit order book is less liquid, has wider spreads, shallower depth, slower post‑shock recovery (often >30 s vs <5 s on NYSE/LSE/JSE) and higher VPIN, indicating more order‑flow toxicity and price‑discovery inefficiency.

## Why it matters (or not)
The study focuses on a frontier equity market and provides no direct insight into NASDAQ/S&P futures, options, or volatility trading.

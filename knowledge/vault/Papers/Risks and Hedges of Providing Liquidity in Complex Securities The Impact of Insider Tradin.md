# Risks and Hedges of Providing Liquidity in Complex Securities: The Impact of Insider Trading on Options Market Makers
☆☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://ir.lawnet.fordham.edu/jcfl/vol15/iss2/2)

## What Volta learned
This 2009 law-journal article by Stanislav Dolgopolov argues that options market makers lose more from insider trading than stock market makers, because they must quote both sides, carry leveraged non-linear positions and hedge their greeks (delta, gamma, vega), so a trader with secret news can pick them off before a big price jump. It reviews US court cases on whether options traders can claim damages and sketches how such losses could be calculated. It is a legal analysis with no market data or numbers.

## Why it matters (or not)

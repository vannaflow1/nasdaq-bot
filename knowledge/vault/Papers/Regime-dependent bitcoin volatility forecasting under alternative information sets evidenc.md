# Regime-dependent bitcoin volatility forecasting under alternative information sets: evidence from econometric, deep learning, and hybrid models
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s42521-026-00229-9.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The paper finds that Bitcoin volatility forecasting accuracy varies by market regime and that LSTM models work best in normal periods, while hybrid GARCH‑LSTM models perform best during crises, with the optimal hybrid depending on the specific crisis.

## Why it matters (or not)
Focuses on Bitcoin, not Nasdaq/S&P futures, and offers limited direct insight for typical retail traders.

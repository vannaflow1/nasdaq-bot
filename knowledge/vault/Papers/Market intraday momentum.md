# Market intraday momentum
★★★★☆  ·  Must-read (curated)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.2440866)

## What Volta learned
The paper 'Market Intraday Momentum' explores the concept of intraday momentum in financial markets, suggesting that returns tend to be positively autocorrelated within a trading day. This means that stocks or indices that experience high returns during the morning tend to continue performing well in the afternoon.

## Why it matters (or not)
This is a well-known paper on market intraday momentum, and its findings have implications for traders and quantitative researchers.

## Concepts
[[intraday momentum]], autocorrelation, [[market microstructure]]

## Idea
A testable trading idea based on this concept is to go long on assets that exhibit strong morning returns, expecting the momentum to persist throughout the day.

# An Empirical Analysis of Stock and Bond Market Liquidity
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.289219)

## What Volta learned
Modelling bid-ask spreads, depth, returns, volatility and order flow in stocks and Treasuries together, the authors find that liquidity and volatility shocks in the two markets are significantly correlated, so common forces drive both. Volatility shocks help predict later changes in liquidity, and in crises monetary easing goes with better liquidity. Money flowing into government bond funds forecasts bond market liquidity. The abstract gives no specific numbers.

## Why it matters (or not)
The idea that volatility spikes come with worse liquidity is useful background for execution, but it is old, cross-market and needs spread/depth data we do not store.

# Duration modelling of the after-hours electronic futures market
★★☆☆☆  ·  Hunt: overnight trading range macroeconomic announcements Nasdaq i  ·  [source](https://eprints.utas.edu.au/14772/2/whole-li-thesis.pdf)

## What Volta learned
The thesis finds that standard ACD models struggle to capture trade duration dynamics in after‑hours futures, but using a Generalized Gamma distribution and allowing for structural breaks (detected via LM tests) improves fit, especially around the 2007‑2008 crisis.

## Why it matters (or not)
Only methodology about trade duration; no direct pricing or return results to apply to current trading.

## Concepts
autoregressive conditional duration, generalized gamma errors, structural break detection, after‑hours futures liquidity, non‑linear duration dynamics

## Idea
Apply LM‑based structural break tests to daily after‑hours trade‑duration series derived from our NQ/GLOBEX data; when a break is detected, switch to a higher‑frequency trading mode (e.g., tighter spreads) for the remainder of the day.

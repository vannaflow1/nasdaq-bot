# The Term Structure of Implied Fee Rates in Liquidity Pools
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://hal.science/hal-05804816)

## What Volta learned
The paper proposes a no‑arbitrage method to infer the implied fee rates that liquidity providers earn in AMM pools, linking fees to the difference between the pool's current value and its risk‑neutral expected payoff.

## Why it matters (or not)
Focuses on crypto AMM pools, not on equity futures/options or Nasdaq/S&P trading.

## Concepts
implied fee rates, liquidity provider compensation, amm no‑arbitrage pricing, stochastic‑volatility jump diffusion, range design impact

# The behavior and performance of major types of futures traders
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/36426/1/MPRA_paper_36426.pdf)

## What Volta learned
Changyun Wang (Journal of Futures Markets, 2003) used the CFTC's Commitments of Traders reports for 15 US futures markets (including S&P 500, T-bond and commodities) from October 1992 to March 2000, measured monthly. Large speculators tended to trade against recent price moves but with market sentiment, while hedgers chased price moves and traded against sentiment. Speculators' position changes lined up with later abnormal returns and hedgers' did not, but the author attributes this to speculators being paid for absorbing hedging pressure rather than to better forecasting.

## Why it matters (or not)
Positioning of speculators vs hedgers can matter for index futures, but the data are monthly, end in 2000 and mostly cover commodities, far from next-day NQ/QQQ decisions.

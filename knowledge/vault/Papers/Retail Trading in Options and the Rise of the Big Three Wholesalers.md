# Retail Trading in Options and the Rise of the Big Three Wholesalers
★★★☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1111/jofi.13285)

## What Volta learned
Retail trading in US options has surged and now exceeds 60% of total market volume, helped by payment for order flow, and nearly 90% of that payment comes from three wholesalers. Using new trade flags to isolate wholesaler-executed trades, the authors build a measure of retail options activity that drops during brokerage outages. Retail traders favor cheap weekly options with an average bid-ask spread of 12.6%, and they lose money on average.

## Why it matters (or not)
Directly relevant to retail QQQ short-dated options traders: wide spreads on cheap weeklies are a big hidden cost, though the paper gives no forecasting signal we can test daily.

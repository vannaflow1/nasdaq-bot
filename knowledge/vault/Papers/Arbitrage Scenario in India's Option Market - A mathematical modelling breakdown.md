# Arbitrage Scenario in India's Option Market - A mathematical modelling breakdown
★☆☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.34492029)

## What Volta learned
Using Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty minute and tick data from January 2025 to April 2026, this self-published paper argues that big institutional selling 'slams' on Wednesday/Thursday expiry afternoons set off retail stop-loss cascades, with a window of about 180-300 seconds between the slam and the peak of the cascade. It claims its 18-model 'Threshold Causal Calculus' suite was right 68.2% of the time during the March 2026 volatility spikes and cut slippage by 85.8% compared with moving-average crossovers. The claims are big, the method is vaguely described, and the comparison is only against a weak baseline.

## Why it matters (or not)
Indian index expiries, intraday tick timing and an unreviewed, loosely specified model; the general idea that stop cascades cluster on expiry days is plausible for QQQ 0DTE, but nothing here transfers directly or can be checked with daily data.

## Concepts
expiry-day gamma trap, stop-loss cascades, intraday price slams, walk-forward backtest, market fragility index

## Idea
Measure intraday stop-cascade windows (180-300 s after a large institutional move) on QQQ expiry afternoons.

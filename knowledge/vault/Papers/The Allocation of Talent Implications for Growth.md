# The Allocation of Talent: Implications for Growth
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (prop mission)  ·  [source](https://dash.harvard.edu/bitstreams/7312037d-e049-6bd4-e053-0100007fdf3b/download)

## What Volta learned
Talent allocation affects growth: when high‑ability people run or organize firms they generate scalable returns, but when they become rent‑seekers they earn high rents without creating value. The sector mix (e.g., engineering vs. law) predicts growth rates.

## Why it matters (or not)
The paper is macro‑economics focused and only indirectly hints at incentives that could shape trader behavior.

## Concepts
talent allocation, entrepreneurial vs. rent‑seeking returns, scale economies, sectoral talent distribution, growth correlation

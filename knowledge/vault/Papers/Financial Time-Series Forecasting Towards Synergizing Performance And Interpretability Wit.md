# Financial Time-Series Forecasting: Towards Synergizing Performance And Interpretability Within a Hybrid Machine Learning Approach
★★☆☆☆  ·  OpenAlex (classic)  ·  [source](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-3825306/v1)

## What Volta learned
The authors compared several hybrid ML models (linear regression variants, LSTM, decision trees) for Bitcoin price forecasting and found that the linear regressor performed best, while also emphasizing interpretability through time‑series preprocessing.

## Why it matters (or not)
Focuses on Bitcoin rather than the Nasdaq/S&P indices and lacks concrete performance numbers for broader markets.

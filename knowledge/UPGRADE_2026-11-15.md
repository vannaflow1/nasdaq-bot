# What changed on 15 Nov 2026 (plain words)

The owner asked for Volta to be upgraded "to its full potential". Honest framing first: no code change makes the
trading edge bigger. What changed is that Volta now tells the truth about itself more reliably, and that two things
that had been broken for three weeks without anyone noticing are fixed.

## Found broken (and fixed)

1. **The strategy search had saved nothing since 25 Oct.** Its two result files reached GitHub's 100 MB limit, so
   every upload was refused - and the job still showed green. The "651,533 tested" number was frozen for three weeks
   while the search kept running 8 times a day. Fix: a full file is parked and a fresh one started (`prop_search.py`).
2. **The upload helper hid failed uploads.** After one refused upload, the retry said "nothing to do" and reported
   success. Fix in `.github/actions_common.sh`.
3. **The evening forward step crashed every night since about 25 Oct** (a missing field), which silently skipped the
   `/strategies` update and the timer. That is why the timer still said "2 forward trades". Fixed, and the timer now
   also covers F13.

## New

- **Watchdog** (`tools/watchdog.py`, hourly): posts to Discord when a job stops running *or* runs without saving its
  results. It would have caught all three problems above within hours.
- **Jobs can no longer overwrite each other's `/command` text** (`tools/status_merge.py`).
- **Weekly scoreboard** (`scoreboard.py`, Sundays, `/scoreboard`): every live claim next to what happened.
- **Self-check** (`tests/`, 54 small tests): runs on every code change and every night.
- **`/bias` is paused** (owner decision): no posts, no LONG/SHORT; it is still graded silently.

## Drafts that need the owner

- `knowledge/proposals/` - two research drafts (options data; a trend/chop filter for the bias). Nothing is registered.
- `ninjatrader/` - a draft SIM execution script. Never compiled, never run. SIM only.

## Not done

- The ~60 top-level modules were not reorganised: no gain worth the risk on a live system.

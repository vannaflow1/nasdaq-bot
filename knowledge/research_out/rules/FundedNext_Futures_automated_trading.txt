At FundedNext Futures, you are allowed to use automated trading systems such as Expert Advisors (EAs) and trading bots during both the Challenge and on FundedNext Accounts. However, we do not offer any support for setting up, configuring, or troubleshooting these systems. Any issues—such as technical errors, execution delays, or system malfunctions—must be managed on your own. FundedNext will not be held responsible for any impact these may have on your trading account. Please remember that while the use of EAs and bots is permitted, strategies that aim to exploit system vulnerabilities—such as latency abuse or order flooding—are strictly prohibited.
